A new Crease every day. Fold carefully.

How to play

  1. Tap a dashed line between two cells to fold the strip there.
  2. The shorter side flips over the longer side. Numbers that land on each other add up.
  3. Match the target using all of your folds. Strips can be flipped, so a reversed match counts.
  4. Running out of folds without a match is a failed attempt. Undo and reset are free.
